## What this portfolio demonstrates

### Evidence policy

- **SOURCE:** Source code or documentation exists; this is not a successful build.
- **BUILD_VERIFIED:** A specific commit builds with a recorded toolchain, dependencies, and reproducible log.
- **KERNEL_AUDITED:** The exact formal theorem and its assumptions/axioms have been inspected.
- **OPEN_MATH:** A mathematical obligation remains unresolved.
- **PUBLICATION_APPROVED:** A separate permission gate; publication does not imply verification.

A successful Lean build alone does not establish an unconditional solution to an open mathematical problem. Conditional theorems must retain their explicit assumptions.
