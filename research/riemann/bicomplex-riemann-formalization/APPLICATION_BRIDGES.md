# Applied systems analyses (items 7–15)

These are independent analyses in chemistry, epidemiology, acoustics, optics, seismology, neurophysiology, aeroelasticity, finance, and network science. They do not infer a Riemann-zeta result.

The 0.3/0.7 pair is a chosen arithmetic encoding, not a measured universal threshold. `Formal/ApplicationBridges.lean` proves only that the selected integers encode that pair. See [cross-domain analysis](CROSS_DOMAIN_ANALYSIS.md), [source materials](SOURCE_MATERIALS.md), and [validation protocols](VALIDATION_PROTOCOLS.md).
